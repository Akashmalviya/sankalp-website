The science, simply

Your body has two modes.
It only heals in one of them.

Your nervous system runs an accelerator and a brake. Modern life — deadlines, screens, late meals, short nights — keeps the accelerator pressed. And a body in stress mode postpones everything else: digestion, deep sleep, repair, even skin and hair.

Stress mode

the accelerator · always on for most of us
  • Heart races, muscles stay tense
  • Digestion slows or turns erratic
  • Sleep gets light and broken
  • Repair — skin, hair, gut — gets postponed

Recovery mode

the brake · where healing actually happens
  • Heart settles, breath deepens
  • Digestion switches fully on
  • Sleep turns deep and restorative
  • The body runs its own repairs

The most overlooked lever? Timing. Many of us eat reasonable food at unreasonable hours, and sleep at hours our body never agreed to. Your plan fixes the when, not just the what.

Every habit in your plan — meal times, a short breathing practice, an evening wind-down — has one job: easing your body off the accelerator, so it can do its own repairs.
