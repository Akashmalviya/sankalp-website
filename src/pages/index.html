Why it keeps coming back

You've probably lived this loop more than once.

1

The report names it

Fatty liver. Prediabetes. PCOS. You get a diagnosis, a prescription, and a ten-minute appointment. Nobody has time to ask how you got here.

2

The medicine holds the line

Numbers steady. Symptoms quieten. But medication manages the condition — it was never designed to undo what caused it.

3

Nothing upstream changed

Same meals at the same wrong hours, same 1am sleep, same unrelenting stress. So the condition holds — or quietly progresses — and the dose goes up.

Lifestyle conditions are built by a lifestyle. Change what built it, and the body gets a real chance to recover.

      Questions

      The things people ask before they call

      Will I have to stop my medicines?
      No — and please don't. We never stop, reduce or change anyone's medication. That decision belongs to your doctor and only your doctor. What we do is work on the lifestyle side so that when you go for your next review, your physician has better numbers to work with. Any change in dosage comes from them, based on those numbers.
      How long before I notice anything?
      Honestly, it depends on what we're fixing. Sleep, energy, bloating and mood usually shift within two to four weeks — those respond fast. Blood markers take longer — typically three to six months of consistent work, and they move at the pace your body decides, not the pace we'd like. Anyone promising faster than that is selling you something.
      What actually happens on the discovery call?
      Thirty minutes, on the phone or WhatsApp. You talk, we listen — what's going on, what you've already tried, what you're hoping for. Then we tell you plainly whether we think we can help and what working together would look like. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you that instead. No cost, no obligation.
      Is this Ayurveda? Is it a diet plan?
      Neither, exactly. It's lifestyle medicine — working on the daily behaviours that drive chronic conditions. We draw on India's traditional understanding of daily living, which got a great deal right about food, timing and rhythm, but we only keep what modern evidence supports. No detoxes, no cleanses, no miracle cures, and nothing you have to believe in for it to work.
      Is it online, or do I need to come in?
      Online. Assessment, protocol and check-ins all happen over call and WhatsApp, so it works wherever you are and fits around your job.
      Do I have to give up everything I like eating?
      No, and plans that demand that are exactly why most people quit by week three. We build around what you already eat and what's actually cooked in your kitchen. Timing and sequencing often matter as much as the food itself — a lot of early progress comes from changing when you eat, not banning what you eat.
      What does it cost?
      We'll walk you through the programmes and pricing on the discovery call, once we know what you actually need — quoting before that would be guesswork. The call itself is free, and there's no pressure to sign up on it.
