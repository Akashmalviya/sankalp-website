Doshas & Prakriti, Explained

What is a dosha?
A dosha is one of three energies Ayurveda uses to describe how your body and mind work — Vata (movement), Pitta (transformation) and Kapha (structure). Everyone has all three; the unique ratio you were born with is your Prakriti.
How do I know which dosha I am?
Take this free 15-question dosha quiz. It looks at your body frame, skin, digestion, sleep, energy and temperament, then shows your dominant dosha and your current balance in about 5 minutes.
What is the difference between Prakriti and Vikriti?
Prakriti is your original, unchanging constitution — the balance you were born with. Vikriti is your current state, which shifts with diet, season, stress and habits. Healing in Ayurveda means gently guiding Vikriti back toward your Prakriti.
Can I have more than one dosha?
Yes. Most people are a blend. When two doshas score close together you have a dual constitution, such as Vata-Pitta or Pitta-Kapha. The quiz shows the full percentage breakdown across all three.
Is this dosha quiz a medical diagnosis?
No. It is an educational self-assessment to help you understand your body's tendencies, not a medical test. For any health condition, please consult a qualified physician or Ayurvedic practitioner.
