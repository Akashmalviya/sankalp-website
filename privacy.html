Sankalp Health

Privacy

Your health is intimate. What you share with Sankalp — your body's patterns, your daily rhythms, your healing journey — is treated with the same reverence a vaidya gives to a patient's trust.

What we collect

🧬
Health Profile

Your Prakriti, Vikriti, and dosha scores derived from your onboarding answers. This is the core of your personalised experience — never sold, never shared with third parties.

📓
Journal Entries

Reflections you write in the Reflect tab. Stored encrypted on our servers. Never used to train AI models without your explicit consent.

📱
Usage Data

Anonymous session and feature-usage signals — which practices you open, how long you spend in a session — to improve recommendations. No personally identifiable information is attached.

📞
Phone Number

Used only for account authentication via OTP. We do not send marketing messages without consent, and we never share your number with advertisers.

How we use it

🌿
Personalisation

Your dosha profile shapes every recommendation — from morning practices to meal guidance — so the app feels like it was made for you, because in every meaningful sense, it is.

🤖
AI Companion

The Vaidya companion uses your health context to give grounded, personalised guidance. Conversations are not used for model training. Your dialogue stays between you and the platform.

📈
Improving Sankalp

Aggregated, anonymised patterns help us understand which practices resonate and which content lands — so we can build better experiences for the entire community.

Your controls

🔄
Retake Your Reading

Your health profile is never permanent. Retake the onboarding assessment any time from the Profile screen to update your Prakriti and Vikriti readings.

📤
Export Your Data

You can request a copy of all data we hold about you. Write to us at privacy@sankalphealth.in and we will send it to you within 30 days.

🗑️
Delete Your Account

You have the right to be forgotten. Request full deletion at any time by writing to privacy@sankalphealth.in. All data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Questions about your privacy, data, or any of the above? We are a small team and we read every message.
