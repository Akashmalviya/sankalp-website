The Problem

We are awake — but lost in thought.

With modernization, we have lost our core. We look awake, but live distracted, never truly present. Comfort has become an addiction — slowly affecting our health through lifestyle and habits.

Instead of fixing the root cause, we suppress symptoms — painkillers for headaches, pills for sleep, antacids for digestion. But the body knows how to heal itself. We have just forgotten how to let it.
